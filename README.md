# I Am an Heir 👑

**Kingdom Declarations for Champions** — a voice-first daily app that helps children declare who they are in Jesus Christ, in their own words, out loud, every day.

> *"...heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ."* — Romans 8:17 (KJV)
> *"For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he."* — Proverbs 23:7 (KJV)

## What it is

A gentle, bright, installable app where each child:

- hears **today's declaration** from a perennial 365-day calendar (same word, same date, every year),
- **says it with me** three times so the Word sinks from the mouth into the heart,
- taps **How I Feel** to find God's Word for a real moment — *scared, sad, a big test, can't sleep, my family & friends, at school,* and more,
- declares **Who I Am** — identity rooted in Christ.

Every one of the **389 declarations** is drawn from and verified against the King James Version of the Holy Bible.

## How to use

- Open the app, add each child by name and choose a buddy.
- Tap **Say it with me** and declare together.
- Grown-ups' settings are behind the parent PIN.

Installable on phones and tablets ("Add to Home Screen"), and works offline.
